Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what homeowners ask us most.

When should I winterize my sprinkler system?

Before temperatures stay below freezing. In the Chicago area that usually means October through early November, but ground temperature drops slower than air temperature, so there is a window. Do not wait for the first hard freeze.

What is a sprinkler blowout?

We connect an air compressor to your system and push the water out of the pipes, valves and heads, one zone at a time. It protects everything from freeze damage.

How long does winterization take?

Usually a short visit. Most homes take well under an hour, depending on how many zones you have.

Do I need to be home?

Usually not, as long as we can reach the controller and shutoff. We will confirm access when we schedule.

What happens if I skip winterization?

Water can freeze and expand inside the system, cracking pipes, valves and sprinkler heads. You often find out in spring when the repair bill shows up.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Contact us and we will set up a time to look at your property.

What areas do you serve?

The Chicagoland area. Reach out with your town and we will confirm we can get to you.

How long does a new installation take?

Most residential systems take a few days depending on yard size and layout. We will give you a clear timeline with your estimate.

My sprinkler head is broken or a zone will not turn on. Can you fix it?

Yes. Repair is a big part of what we do. Tell us what you are seeing when you contact us.

Do I need a backflow test?

Many towns require backflow preventer testing on irrigation systems. Requirements vary by municipality, so check with your village or ask us when you book.
