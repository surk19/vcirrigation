Our services

Everything your sprinkler system needs, from the first trench to the last blowout.

New system installation

We design the system around your yard instead of forcing a template onto it.

  • Layout and zone design for lawn and planting beds
  • Head and nozzle selection for even coverage
  • Controller setup and a walkthrough on how to use it
  • Clean install with the lawn restored

Service and repair

Something not working right? We find the cause and fix it properly.

  • Broken or misaligned heads and nozzles
  • Leaks, low pressure and zones that will not turn on
  • Valve and wiring troubleshooting
  • Timer and controller replacement

Spring startup

A quick check in spring catches small problems before they cost you a dry summer.

  • Turn the system on and test every zone
  • Look for leaks and damaged heads
  • Adjust spray patterns and program the schedule

Fall winterization

In our climate the frost goes deeper than the pipes. Water left inside can freeze and crack lines, valves and heads.

  • Compressed air blowout of every zone
  • Shut down and drain the system
  • Controller set to rain mode for winter
  • Best done before temperatures stay below freezing

Upgrades and add-ons

Make an older system more efficient.

  • Smart controllers and rain sensors
  • Drip zones for gardens and beds
  • Adding zones for new landscaping
